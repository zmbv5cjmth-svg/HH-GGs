The ones people miscount

Double run

4♥ 5♠ 5♦ 6♣ J♠

Each 5 makes its own 4‑5‑6, so that's two runs of three = 6. Pair of fives 2. Fifteens: 4+5+6 twice 4, 5+J twice 4. Total 16.

Three of a kind in a run

6♠ 7♥ 7♦ 7♣ 8♠

Three separate 6‑7‑8 runs = 9. Three of a kind 6. Fifteens: 7+8 three times 6. Total 21.

Don't memorize totalsWalk the five categories in order every single time. Fifteens, pairs, runs, flush, nobs. It's slower for a week and then it isn't.

How runs build

Order played doesn't matter

5♠ 3♥ 4♦ run of 3 → 3

3‑4‑5 rearranged is a sequence, so the player of the 4 pegs 3.

Runs keep extending

5♠ 3♥ 4♦ 2♣ run of 4 → 4

The next player extends it and takes the whole longer run.

A duplicate breaks it

5♠ 3♥ 3♦ 4♣ no run

The second 3 is a pair for 2. After it, no run can include both threes, so the 4 scores nothing.

The go, precisely

Say "go" when no card in your hand fits under 31. Your opponent keeps laying cards as long as they can — scoring pairs, runs, and fifteens normally. When neither of you can play, the last player to lay a card pegs 1 (or 2 for hitting 31 exactly). Then the count resets to zero and the player who did not lay the last card leads the next round.
